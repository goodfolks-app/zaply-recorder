Why a recorder?

iPhones and Apple TVs can't record in the background: the system suspends the app. Zaply Recorder does it at home instead, so a match or a whole series is recorded while your devices sleep.

Record anything

A channel now, a programme from the TV guide, every airing of a show, or a match.

Download films and series

To your library at home, as the provider's own file, with every audio and subtitle track.

Watch while it records

From any Zaply device, at the start or at the live edge.

Respects your line

Never more connections than your provider allows. When the line is busy, it records the channel's other copy: HD, FHD or 4K.

One file per recording

In folders Plex, Jellyfin and Emby read as they are.

Alerts

Zaply tells you when a recording is ready, or when one failed.

Away from home

Through Tailscale, or a forwarded HTTPS port with the recorder's own certificate.

Only through a VPN

If you want, it records only while the computer's VPN is up.

Set it up

A few minutes, once.

  1. Install it on the computer that stays on, from the downloads above.
  2. Open its page, http://<the computer>:9393, to set a password and choose where recordings go. The Mac and Windows apps open it from their menu.
  3. In Zaply: Settings ▸ Zaply Recorder ▸ Set up your recorder. Zaply finds it on your network by itself.
  4. Record from a channel, the TV guide or a match, and download a film or an episode with Download to the recorder. They show in Zaply, under Films and Series.

Away from home

Tailscale

Recommended. Install Tailscale on the recorder's computer and on your iPhone, then give Zaply the recorder's Tailscale address in Settings ▸ Zaply Recorder ▸ Address away from home. Nothing is exposed to the internet.

Port forwarding

Forward the HTTPS port, 9394, on your router (never 9393), and give Zaply https://<your home's address>:9394. Zaply trusts the recorder's own certificate, learnt at home.
